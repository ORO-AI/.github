# ORO AI

ORO AI is an applied AI research lab building simulated businesses where models can practice making decisions and learn from their consequences. We believe AI will learn to run entire businesses.

Our initial offering is business environments for model builders to evaluate and train autonomous agents. The goal is judgment that transfers across knowledge work, so models can take responsibility for a business function and eventually an entire business. We study capability and alignment together: whether agents make better decisions while following constraints and pursuing the intended goal.

## Research

**SMB Bench** measures whether AI agents learn from the delayed consequences of their own decisions. It puts an agent in charge of a simulated produce wholesaler's buying desk for a 90-day quarter; performance is measured by quarterly profit and compared with a fixed buying rule.
